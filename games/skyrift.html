Rider briefing

How to ride

Controls

A DSteer. In the air, spin the bike. SpaceJump. Hold to float further. Shift + steerDrift. Release for a boost. WBoost (uses the boost meter) SBrake Click / JFire the blaster (it aims itself) Esc / PPause · M mutes

Staying alive

  • There are no walls on most of the track. Steer into bends or you slide off the edge.
  • Drift sparks go blue, then orange, then purple. Hold longer for a bigger boost.
  • Land spins straight: 360° and 720° fill your boost meter. Crooked landings cost speed.
  • Jump laser gates and shockwaves. Shoot barriers, mines and drones out of your way.
  • The Warden appears every 2.7 km. Break both shoulder cores, then the chest core.
  • Chaining pickups, tricks and near misses raises your score multiplier, up to x5.
