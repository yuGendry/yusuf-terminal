# Advanced game reviews

# Scores are out of 10 (0 = not applicable). Hours are approximate time to finish the main content.
Stardew Valley|9|7|8|9|10|10|50–100 h|Easy|Up to 8-player online co-op; 4-player split screen on consoles|Anyone who wants a relaxing routine with long-term goals|You need constant action or a strong main story|The genius of Stardew is how it layers systems without ever forcing them on you. Farming pays for tools, tools open the mines, the mines feed crafting, and crafting automates the farm so you have time for festivals and friendships. The 1.6 update added a new farm type, late-game content and hundreds of small touches. It runs on anything, and the co-op is one of the best ways to share a game with a partner.
Celeste|10|8|8|10|10|9|8–10 h main, 40+ h for everything|Hard (Assist Mode available)|Single-player|Players who love precise controls and satisfying mastery|You get frustrated by repeated deaths|Every screen is a short, self-contained challenge, and restarts are instant, so failure never costs you time. The dash has a few hidden techniques that the game slowly teaches, and the optional strawberries and B-sides push them to the limit. Lena Raine's soundtrack is outstanding. The free Farewell chapter is one of the hardest and most beautiful levels in any platformer.
Hollow Knight|9|7|9|10|9|10|30–40 h, 60+ h for 100%|Hard|Single-player|Explorers who enjoy getting lost and finding secrets|You hate backtracking or unclear directions|Hallownest rewards patience. You buy maps from a cartographer who hides in each area, charms let you tune your build, and dozens of optional bosses hide in the corners. Its melancholy atmosphere and Christopher Larkin's score make it one of the most memorable worlds in indie games. It is enormous for its price, which is both a gift and a warning.
Hollow Knight: Silksong|9|7|10|10|9|10|30–45 h|Hard|Single-player|Fans of Hollow Knight who want more speed and challenge|The first game's difficulty already felt like too much|Hornet's silk abilities and tools make combat faster and more aggressive than the original, and healing now happens in one quick burst that encourages risk. Pharloom is a vertical kingdom with quests from bug citizens, which gives exploration more direction. The early hours are demanding, and some runbacks to bosses feel long, but the art and music are extraordinary.
Baldur's Gate 3|10|10|9|10|7|10|75–120 h|Adjustable (Explorer to Honour Mode)|Up to 4-player online co-op; 2-player split screen on consoles|RPG fans who love choices, tactics and strong characters|You dislike turn-based combat or long games|Almost every problem has several solutions: talk your way through, sneak, shove an enemy off a ledge, or turn into a bear. Companions such as Astarion, Shadowheart and Karlach react to what you do and can leave or betray you. Act 3's city is dense and can strain older hardware, though patches have improved performance a lot. It is one of the most complete RPGs ever made.
It Takes Two|10|7|9|8|9|9|12–15 h|Medium|2-player only, online or couch co-op (Friend's Pass)|Couples and friends looking for a shared adventure|You want to play alone|The game never repeats itself: one level gives one player a hammer and the other nails, the next turns you into bees, and a later chapter becomes a top-down shooter. Every mechanic needs both players to cooperate. The story about a divorcing couple is cheesy at times, but the gameplay variety is unmatched.
Split Fiction|10|7|10|9|9|9|14–17 h|Medium|2-player only, online or couch co-op (Friend's Pass, cross-play)|Co-op partners who loved It Takes Two|You want a deep story|Split Fiction constantly swaps between sci-fi and fantasy worlds and gives each player different abilities. Side stories last about ten minutes each and parody whole genres, from racing to 2D platformers. Cross-play and the Friend's Pass make it easy to find a partner. The characters are likeable but the plot plays it safe.
Minecraft|10|3|7|8|8|10|Endless|Easy to Hard (you choose)|Online servers, Realms, up to 4-player split screen on consoles|Builders, explorers and families|You need clear goals and a story|Survival mode gives you a loop of gathering, crafting and surviving the night, while Creative mode is pure building. Redstone circuits let players make working machines and even computers. Bedrock edition runs on nearly everything with cross-play; Java edition on PC has the best mods. Regular free updates keep adding new biomes and mobs.
Outer Wilds|10|10|8|10|8|10|15–20 h, plus 5 h expansion|Medium|Single-player|Curious players who love mysteries and discovery|You want combat, loot or upgrades|The whole solar system runs on a 22-minute clock, with planets that change over time: one is being swallowed by sand, another is falling apart. You piece together what happened to an ancient race by exploring and reading. The Echoes of the Eye expansion adds a spooky new mystery. It is best played completely blind, so avoid spoilers.
Balatro|10|0|8|9|10|10|20–100+ h|Medium|Single-player|Card-game fans and anyone who loves chasing high scores|Randomness in card games annoys you|Balatro uses poker hands as a scoring system, then lets you break it with 150 Jokers that multiply points in wild ways. Each run is short, and unlocking decks and stakes gives long-term goals. It plays perfectly with a controller, mouse or touch. Free updates have added new Jokers and collaborations with other games.
Disco Elysium|8|10|9|10|9|10|25–35 h|Medium|Single-player|Players who love great writing and choice-driven stories|You don't enjoy reading lots of text|You solve a murder in a broken city while your own skills argue with you in the dialogue. Failed checks lead to hilarious or tragic outcomes, and your political and personal choices shape who your detective becomes. The Final Cut version is fully voiced. It is one of the best-written games ever made.
Portal 2|10|9|8|9|10|10|8–10 h, plus 6 h co-op|Medium|2-player co-op campaign (online and split screen)|Puzzle fans and anyone who enjoys comedy|You want action and combat|Each chamber introduces a new idea, such as gels that make you bounce or light bridges, then combines them in clever ways. Stephen Merchant and J.K. Simmons join GLaDOS for some of the funniest writing in games. The co-op campaign has its own robots, puzzles and gestures. Still cheap and runs on almost any PC.
Unpacking|8|8|9|9|10|8|3–4 h|Easy|Single-player|Players who want something calm and thoughtful|You want a long game|You unpack a woman's belongings across eight homes between 1997 and 2018, and the items tell her story: what she keeps, what she hides, what she leaves behind. It is a gentle puzzle with no timer. The pixel art and sound effects are wonderful.
A Short Hike|9|7|8|9|10|10|2–4 h|Easy|Single-player|Anyone looking for a warm, short game|You need lots of content|Hawk Peak Provincial Park is small but full of secrets, characters and mini-games. Collecting golden feathers lets you climb and glide further. It is a perfect first game for non-gamers and a lovely palate cleanser for experienced players.
Dredge|8|8|8|9|9|8|10–12 h|Easy to Medium|Single-player|Players who like atmospheric, slightly scary games|Repetition bothers you|By day you catch fish and upgrade your boat. At night, panic rises and strange things happen. Some fish are twisted into horrible shapes, and the mystery slowly unfolds. The DLCs add more areas.
Resident Evil 4|10|8|10|9|9|10|16–20 h|Medium to Hard|Single-player (free Mercenaries mode)|Horror and action fans|Gore and jump scares are too much|The 2023 remake keeps the structure of the classic while rebuilding everything. The knife parry adds a new defensive tool, and Ashley and Luis are better characters. The Separate Ways DLC is excellent.
Alan Wake 2|8|10|10|10|7|9|18–22 h|Medium|Single-player|Players who love strange, cinematic stories|You want fast action|Saga Anderson's investigation and Alan's trapped writing blend into one mystery. The Mind Place and Writer's Room let you rewrite scenes. Visuals are stunning on high-end PCs and PS5.
Lethal Company|9|3|6|8|8|10|Endless|Medium|Up to 4 players online (more with mods)|Groups of friends who love chaotic, funny horror|You mostly play alone|You land on abandoned moons, grab scrap and haul it back before the ship leaves, all to meet an ever-rising company quota. Proximity voice chat is the secret ingredient: you hear friends' voices fade as they wander off, and their screams cut out mid-sentence. Monsters are simple but unpredictable, so every trip becomes a story. It is still in early access, and the modding scene adds huge amounts of content.
Overcooked! 2|9|3|7|8|9|8|8–10 h campaign|Medium|Up to 4 players, online or couch|Families and friend groups who like a bit of stress|Your group argues easily under pressure|Kitchens split apart, drift on rafts or catch fire while you chop, cook and plate orders against the clock. Throwing ingredients across the room was new to this sequel and makes teamwork much more fluid. Levels are only a few minutes long, so it is easy to play a few rounds. Solo play, where you swap between two chefs, is noticeably weaker.
Mario Kart 8 Deluxe|10|0|9|9|10|9|Endless|Easy to Hard|Up to 4-player split screen, 12 players online|Everyone, especially families and parties|You want a realistic racing simulation|With the Booster Course Pass there are 96 courses, the largest selection in any Mario Kart. Assist options like smart steering and auto-accelerate let young kids finish races, while 200cc mode and time trials challenge experts. Items keep races close, which is great at parties and occasionally maddening. Battle mode is still the weakest part.
Mario Kart World|9|0|10|10|9|8|Endless|Easy to Hard|Up to 4-player split screen, 24 players online|New Switch 2 owners and party players|You mainly want lots of classic tracks|Races flow from one course to the next across a single connected map, and 24 racers make every Grand Prix hectic. Knockout Tour eliminates the back of the pack at checkpoints over a long cross-country route, and it is the best new mode in years. New tricks like grinding rails and wall-riding add depth. Free Roam has hidden challenges but can feel empty between them.
Vampire Survivors|9|0|6|8|8|10|15–60 h|Easy|Up to 4-player couch co-op|Anyone who wants simple, satisfying fun in short bursts|Screen-filling chaos bothers you|You only control movement; your weapons fire on their own, and you pick upgrades as you level. Combining certain weapons and items evolves them into screen-clearing forms, and discovering these combos is the real game. There are hundreds of secrets and unlockable characters. Paid DLCs add worlds based on games like Castlevania.
Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|10|8|9|10|8|10|60–150 h|Medium|Single-player|Creative players and open-world explorers|You didn't enjoy Breath of the Wild's freedom|Ultrahand lets you glue objects together into cars, planes and absurd contraptions, while Fuse sticks items onto weapons and arrows. The sky islands above and the vast, dark Depths below turn Hyrule into three connected maps. Temples are better than the previous game's Divine Beasts. On Switch 2 it runs at a higher resolution and a smoother frame rate.
Zelda: Breath of the Wild|10|7|9|10|8|10|50–150 h|Medium|Single-player|Anyone who loves exploration and discovery|Constantly breaking weapons annoys you|You can climb any surface and glide from any height, so the whole map is open from the start. Shrines and Korok seeds reward curiosity everywhere, and physics let you solve problems your own way, like setting grass on fire to create updrafts. The two DLC packs add trial modes and a motorcycle. On Switch 2 it runs at a higher resolution and smoother frame rate.
Animal Crossing: New Horizons|8|3|9|9|9|9|Endless|Easy|Up to 4 players on one Switch, up to 8 online|Relaxed players who enjoy a daily routine|You want clear goals and fast progress|The game runs on the real clock and calendar, so seasons, holidays and shops change over the year. Terraforming lets you reshape the whole island, and decorating outdoors was a big step forward for the series. The Happy Home Paradise DLC adds a full home-design mode for villagers. Crafting menus are slow and you can only craft one item at a time.
Hades II|10|9|10|10|9|10|30–60 h|Hard (God Mode available)|Single-player|Action fans and roguelike players|You dislike repeating runs|Melinoë's magic, Omega moves and her ability to cast circles on the ground make combat more tactical than in Hades. There are two routes to explore, up to the surface and down through the Underworld, each with different bosses. Between runs you gather ingredients, perform incantations and spend time with a large cast. The writing and voice acting are, again, superb.
Hades|10|9|10|10|10|10|20–60 h|Medium to Hard (God Mode available)|Single-player|New roguelike players and action fans|You dislike repetition|Every escape attempt gives you blessings from Olympian gods, and combining them with six weapon types creates endless builds. Elden Ring|10|7|9|9|7|10|60–150 h|Very hard|Online co-op and PvP|Players who want a huge challenge and a world to explore|You don't want to die many times|The Lands Between is enormous, and nearly every cave, castle and catacomb holds a new weapon, spell or boss. Spirit Ashes let you summon help, and summoning other players makes tough bosses easier. The story is told through item descriptions and hints, so it rewards players who dig. The Shadow of the Erdtree expansion adds a second, equally large world. PC performance has stutters.
Elden Ring Nightreign|8|5|8|9|8|8|30–60 h|Hard|3-player co-op (also solo and duos)|Friends who want fast Souls-style co-op|You mainly play solo|Each run lasts three in-game days on a shrinking map, and the third night ends with a big boss. Nightfarers each have unique skills and ultimates, so team composition matters. Map events change the landscape between runs. Solo play is noticeably harder and less fun, and matchmaking with strangers without voice chat is tough.

The story takes bold turns, and Lorien Testard's soundtrack is one of the best in years. Maps are sometimes hard to read.
God of War Ragnarök|9|9|10|10|9|9|25–40 h|Adjustable|Single-player|Action fans who love story|You want an open world|Kratos and Atreus travel to all nine realms as Ragnarök approaches. Ghost of Yōtei|9|9|10|10|9|9|25–40 h|Adjustable|Single-player at launch|Fans of Ghost of Tsushima|You are tired of open-world structures|Atsu hunts the Yōtei Six, the gang that killed her family, around Mount Yōtei in 1603.
