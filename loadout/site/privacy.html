Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what Loadout, run by YOUR_NAME, collects and why. Short version: we keep only what's needed to run your account and Pro, we don't sell your data, and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture. These are handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Your library and ratings: if you're on Pro, the games and apps you track and your ratings and notes are stored in Google Firebase so they sync between devices. Without Pro they stay in your browser.
  • Payments: Stripe processes Pro payments. We never see or store your card number. We keep your Stripe customer ID and whether your subscription is active.
  • On your device: your browser stores your settings (like filters) and, without Pro, your ratings.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your data, we don't run ads, and we don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Who processes it

Google Firebase (sign-in, database and hosting) and Stripe (payments). They process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms. Links to other stores take you to their sites, which have their own policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data while your account exists. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email YOUR_EMAIL@example.com from the address on your account. Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires.

Children

Loadout isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email YOUR_EMAIL@example.com and we'll help.
