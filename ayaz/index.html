Before you ask

Delivery

Orders are dispatched from England within 2 working days. UK: 1 to 3 working days, free over £150. Europe: 3 to 6 working days. Rest of the world: 6 to 12 working days. Duties are shown at checkout.

Returns

You have 14 days from delivery to send a piece back, unworn with tags attached. Drop pieces are not restocked, so exchanges depend on what is left in your size.

Sizing

Tops are cut boxy with a dropped shoulder. Take your usual size for the intended fit, or go one down for a closer one. Every product page has measurements in centimetres.

Care

Wash cold, inside out. Hang dry. Do not iron the print. It is cold enough out there, the tumble dryer can stay off.
